Safety

My rules, for your peace of mind.

«Fake technician» scams are real, and they use programs like AnyDesk. Here's how to tell you're in the right place.

I only contact you if you ask me to

No surprise calls, no fake alarms on your screen.

I never ask for your bank codes

Passwords, PINs, OTP codes and card details stay yours alone.

No gift cards or crypto

You pay at the end of the session, with traceable methods and an invoice.

I don't «recover» scammed money

Anyone who promises that is a scammer. If you've been scammed, report it to the Italian Postal Police.

You open and close the session

When the job is done, access ends. No permanent access without your consent.

I only touch what's needed

I don't open personal files the job doesn't need, and I don't copy your data.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know.

Is it safe to let someone into my computer?

Yes, if you know who they are. The AnyDesk connection is encrypted, it only starts when you accept it, and you see everything I do. You can close it at any moment with one click. Also read my safety rules.

What do I need to get started?

A computer or smartphone that's switched on and connected to the internet, and AnyDesk downloaded from the official website. If you're not sure how, I'll guide you over the phone.

How long does a session take?

Many simple problems are fixed in under half an hour. For more complex cases I give you an estimate before we start, and if more time is needed I ask you before carrying on.

What if the problem can't be fixed?

If the problem can't be fixed remotely, you don't pay. I'll honestly tell you what to do and who to turn to.

How do I pay? Do I get an invoice?

You pay at the end of the session by card, PayPal, Satispay or instant bank transfer. You always get an electronic invoice.

Do you also work on Mac, iPhone and Android?

Yes. On Mac and Android I can control the device remotely. On iPhone and iPad I see the screen you share and guide you step by step.

Can I do something else while you work?

Yes. For long tasks, like updates or backups, you can leave the computer on: I'll message you when I'm done. Access only lasts for that session.

What is the PC monitoring included in the subscriptions?

A small program, installed only with your consent, that sends me your computer's health once a day: disk space, antivirus, updates and backups. If something is wrong, I warn you before it becomes a problem. It doesn't read your files and can't be used to control your PC.

What are the terms of service?

Prices, payments, the «not fixed, not paid» guarantee and your right of withdrawal are explained in the terms of service, in plain words.

Do you work with businesses and freelancers?

Yes, with the Professional subscription or a custom plan. If your work involves customer data, we sign the data processor agreement required by the GDPR. Discover support for businesses.
