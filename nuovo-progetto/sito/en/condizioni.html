For packs and subscriptions, withdrawal within 14 days entitles the customer to a refund of the unused part.

7. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are monthly and renew automatically. They can be cancelled at any time, with no penalty, effective from the following month. Sessions included in one month don't carry over to the following months.

8. Computer monitoring

For subscribers who request it, the technician installs a small program that sends the computer's health once a day: disk space, antivirus, firewall, updates, disk health and the date of the last backup. The program doesn't read personal files, doesn't record what the user does and can't be used to control the computer remotely. You can ask for it to be removed at any time; when the subscription ends it is deactivated and uninstalls itself.

Customers who leave an email address receive a receipt for their request and, for appointments, a confirmation and a reminder. On WhatsApp, automatic messages are only sent if the customer asked for them by ticking the box, and there are at most three per request: confirmation, reminder and review request. The review request by email is also only sent with that consent.
