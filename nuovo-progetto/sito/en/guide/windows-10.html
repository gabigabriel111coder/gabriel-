Windows 10 without updates: what to do now

Since 14 October 2025, Windows 10 no longer receives free updates. Your PC keeps working, but it becomes more and more exposed. Here are the three possible options.

Updated September 2026

What has changed

Microsoft ended support for Windows 10 on 14 October 2025. Your computer turns on and works as before, but without security updates every new flaw stays open: over time it becomes easier for a virus to infect it.

Option 1: move to Windows 11

If your PC is compatible, the upgrade to Windows 11 is free and keeps your files and programs. Among other things you need a supported processor, 4 GB of memory, 64 GB of storage and the TPM 2.0 security chip. Microsoft's PC Health Check app tells you in a minute whether your PC is compatible.

There are tricks to install Windows 11 on incompatible PCs: I don't recommend them, because the system may stop receiving updates or work badly.

Option 2: Extended Security Updates (ESU)

If your PC isn't compatible, Microsoft offers individuals the ESUprogramme: security updates for a limited period. It can be enabled for free by signing in with a Microsoft account. Conditions and deadlines may change: I'll help you check whether your PC can enable it, and until when.

Option 3: change computer

If your PC is many years old and slow, sometimes it's worth replacing it. In that case I'll help you choose without spending more than necessary and transfer all your data from the old one to the new one, remotely.
